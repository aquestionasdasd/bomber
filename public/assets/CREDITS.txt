hirakata-complete-model.js / hirakata-complete.glb
Original scripted station geometry created for this project from the owner's
walking-video references, 2026-11-02. Dimensions and unseen details are estimates;
this is not a measured survey or a photogrammetry reconstruction.
Source GLB SHA256: 3769f66622423958d10e58f22bc2ad9aa2aecd9284e07a57170c28cd11d0b800
2026-11-03: the T-SITE deck has a stair opening, a landing and two side walks.
Only its five floor-material mesh payloads changed; the original reference
model is retained in the project history. See prepare-hirakata-stair-opening.py.
2026-11-03: the unsupported third terrace cafe setting and paving rings were
moved onto the existing terrace floor. Only four TSITE_terrace mesh payloads
changed; walking/collision layout and the stair opening are unchanged.
See prepare-hirakata-terrace-support.py for the reproducible source preparation.
2026-11-03: Blender bevels the vertical edges of 1,430 selected boxes by at most
2cm, preserving floors, stairs, contact heights, walking layout and module order.
See scripts/prepare-hirakata-district-geometry.py. The original protected mesh
payloads and bounded edge changes are checked by the district geometry tests.
The binary includes geometry and authored PBR material parameters. It includes
no reference video frames, recordings, texture library, or font files.
Station label outlines were baked from locally installed Meiryo / Yu Gothic.
The game removes six static display buses in favor of its four interactive buses.
The three modeled taxis remain. Reflections use a procedural local light cube.

hirakata-oak-finish.js
Oak Veneer 01 by Jenelle van Heerden, Poly Haven, CC0-1.0.
Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01
License: https://polyhaven.com/license
Prepared from the 1k JPG diffuse, roughness and OpenGL normal maps: resized to
512px, diffuse converted to neutral luminance to retain authored surface colors,
roughness range adjusted, normals renormalized, rows reversed for DataTexture.
The three maps are bundled locally; gameplay makes no texture-provider request.
Source and prepared-byte hashes are embedded in the asset provenance.
See scripts/prepare-hirakata-oak-finish.py. Plaster, brushed-metal, leaf-cluster
finishes and contact AO are original deterministic procedural textures.

hirakata-district-finish.js
Three prepared texture families from Poly Haven, CC0-1.0.
Clean Asphalt - Dimitrios Savva
https://polyhaven.com/a/clean_asphalt
Granular Concrete - Dario Barresi (processing), Michael Jenkins (photography)
https://polyhaven.com/a/granular_concrete
Granite Tile - Charlotte Baglioni
https://polyhaven.com/a/granite_tile
License: https://polyhaven.com/license
Prepared diffuse, roughness and OpenGL normal maps at 512px. Albedo uses
neutral modulation; normals are renormalized and stored bottom-up.
Granite uses a joint-free crop with matched repeat edges.
Source/prepared hashes are embedded in provenance. Maps are bundled locally;
gameplay makes no provider request.
See scripts/prepare-hirakata-district-materials.py.
